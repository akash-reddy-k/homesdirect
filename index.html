HomesDirect

Rent directly from owners. Free for everyone.

HomesDirect is a property marketplace for Hyderabad where owners list homes for free and tenants and buyers contact them directly: call, WhatsApp or chat. No brokers, no brokerage, and no paywall in front of an owner's phone number.

  • Free to list. Owners post a rental or sale with photos and a walkthrough video at no cost.
  • Free to contact. Browse without an account and reach the owner straight away.
  • Owners only. Listings that people report as brokers are hidden for review.

Status

HomesDirect is in early development. The Android app isn't public yet.
