Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

This policy explains what HomesDirect (the Android app and this website) collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we collect what's needed to list homes and connect people with owners. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign up with email, we store your email and a securely hashed password. Browsing and calling owners don't need an account.
  • Listings you post. Property details, price, address, the map location you pin, photos and, if you choose to show it, a contact phone number. Listings are public. Location data embedded in photos (EXIF/GPS) is removed on your phone before upload.
  • Walkthrough videos. If you add a video, it is uploaded to the HomesDirect YouTube channel as an unlisted video and embedded in your listing. Our copy is deleted once YouTube has it. Videos on YouTube are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Chats. Messages between you and an owner, stored so they can be delivered and shown to both of you.
  • Contact and view counts. When someone taps Call or WhatsApp on a listing, we record which listing and either the signed-in account or a random ID created on the device (not linked to your identity). We count listing views. We use these to show owners how their listing is doing and to measure whether HomesDirect is useful.
  • Service requests. If you ask for help (for example a rental agreement or a home loan), we store your phone number and request so we can contact you about it.
  • Reports. If you report a listing, we store the report and your account.
  • Server logs. Our server keeps basic request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and to stop abuse.

Who we share it with

  • Listings, including any phone number you choose to show, are public.
  • Walkthrough videos are hosted by YouTube (Google).
  • For a service request, we share your details with a partner only to provide the service you asked for, and we tell you who it is first.
  • We disclose information if the law requires it.

We don't sell or rent personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Listings stop showing after 45 days (rentals) or 120 days (sales) unless the owner renews them.
  • Photos and the video of a listing that has been rented, sold or expired for 60 days are deleted, including the video on YouTube. Removing a video from a listing also deletes it from YouTube.
  • Account data stays until you ask us to delete your account. Deleting your account deletes your listings, chats and requests.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete your listings in the app at any time. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account, email homesdirectplatform@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. This contact also handles grievances under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is limited to the people running HomesDirect.

Children

HomesDirect is for adults renting, buying or selling property, and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. For significant changes we'll tell you in the app.
